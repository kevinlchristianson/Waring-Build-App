Notes

  1. Plan coordinates: x runs east from the outside face of the west wall framing, y runs north from the outside face of the front wall framing; the grid is 5'. The front of the house is drawn facing south: confirm the lot orientation.
  2. Blue dimensions are each room's clear size, stud face to stud face: exterior walls to the inside face of the 2x6 studs, interior walls to the stud face 1-3/4" off the centre line.
  3. Ceilings are 9'-0" flat throughout, including the garage, on 104-5/8" precut studs with a PT bottom plate and a double top plate.
  4. The roof is a simple 6:12 gable, ridge east–west: trusses clear-span 40' from wall 1 to wall 3, so walls 1 and 3 bear and every interior wall is a partition. Headers in walls 1 and 3 are sized for the truss load; walls 2 and 4 are gable ends.
  5. Foundation: slab on grade, thickened edge or frost-protected stem wall to the local frost depth; 10-mil vapour retarder; R-10 under-slab perimeter insulation 4' in (IECC climate zone 6 assumed). Garage slab 4" below the house slab, sloped 1/8"/ft to the door.
  6. Garage separation (R302.6): 1/2" gypsum on the garage side of walls 13 and 14, 5/8" Type X on the garage ceiling; D3 is 20-minute rated and self-closing. Seal every penetration in those walls.
  7. Door rough openings: prehung interior 2" over the leaf width x 82-1/2"; exterior per the door maker. Window rough openings 1/2" over the unit size; heads at 8'-0".
  8. Bedroom windows N1, N4, N6, N7 and N9 are egress windows: 5.7 sq ft clear opening, 20" min width, 24" min height, sill 44" max (R310). Choose a unit size that meets the clear opening.
