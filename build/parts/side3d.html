Reading it

The plan view puts north (the rear) at the top, like the drawings: the porch and garage door face south. Click anything to read what it is: walls carry their S-1 number, devices their E-1 tag, pipes and ducts their size and run. Drains sit under the slab and rise into the walls; vents and every duct run in the attic to FAU-1, the gas furnace over the back hall; gas comes in at the meter on the garage east wall, and the mechanical room is in the garage.
